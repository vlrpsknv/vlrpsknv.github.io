Sabka

One subscription to 50+ AI models for people in Russia, paid in rubles. I co-founded it and rebuilt the product in 11 weeks with a team of AI agents.

Company
Sabka
Years
2025–now
Role
Co-founder, CPO
Scope
Product, Brand, UX/UI, Frontend, Backend, AI gateway, Infrastructure
  • 52AI models in one chat
  • 11 weeksFrom the decision to rebuild to the night it went live
  • 1 nightTo move the live product to the new platform
  • 43Production releases between September 27 and November 3

One window for every model

Strong AI models are hard to use from Russia. You need a VPN, a foreign card and several subscriptions, and you still have to guess which model fits the task.

Sabka puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and image and video models into one chat, paid in rubles. It reads the request and picks the model. People can also choose it themselves, and they see the price before they send.

Answers you can check

Multichat sends one question to three models and puts the answers side by side.

Files are checked before delivery: a vision model looks at the finished document and sends it back if something is off.

One memory for every model

Sabka keeps one memory per person. A fact from one conversation carries over to the next and to every text model: what you tell GPT, Claude already knows.

Wind in the empty chat

Before the first message the chat shows a field of glyphs with wind running through it. I drew it in WebGL. A fast pointer leaves a warm trail.

For teams

The same product is sold to companies: one subscription for the team, payment by invoice, data stored in Russia. I designed and built the business page; its first screen plays a real task, a spreadsheet and a deck made from one request.

Dark and light themes share one token set. I moved the accent from neon #4AFFA4 to #35D586 and raised muted text from 4.26:1 to 4.61:1, past WCAG AA.

Rebuilt with AI agents

In July 2026 we decided to rebuild Sabka from scratch. I designed the product and the architecture and ran up to ten Claude Code sessions in parallel. Each had its own role, and a second agent reviewed every change before it merged.

On September 28 we moved the live product and its database overnight. Fourteen agents checked production that night. 43 production releases shipped between September 27 and November 3.
