The problem

Strong AI models are hard to use from Russia. You need a VPN, a foreign card and several subscriptions, and you still have to guess which model fits the task.

One window for every model

Sabka puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and image and video models into one chat, paid in rubles. A planner reads the request and picks the model.

People can also choose the model themselves, and they see the price before they send.

The landing shows the whole range in one block: a cat drawn in the Images card is dragged into the Video card and comes to life there.

Answers you can check

Multichat sends one question to three models and shows the answers side by side. Factcheck returns a verdict with sources.

Documents, slides and spreadsheets are checked before delivery: a vision model looks at the finished file and sends it back for another pass if something is off.

One memory for every model

Sabka remembers the person, not the chat. Facts from one conversation carry over to the next, and to every text model: what you tell GPT, Claude already knows.

On the landing, that memory is a cube of facts you can turn by hand. Hover a point to see what it links to, click it to read the fact.

For teams

The same product is sold to companies: one subscription for the whole team, payment by invoice and data stored in Russia. The business page shows the product at work instead of describing it.

Two themes, one system

Dark and light themes come from one set of tokens. I moved the accent from neon #4AFFA4 to #35D586 and raised muted text from 4.26:1 to WCAG AA.

Rebuilt with AI agents

In July 2026 we decided to rebuild Sabka from scratch on one stack. I designed the product and the architecture and ran up to ten Claude Code sessions in parallel, each with its own role and an adversarial review before anything merged. About 70% of my commits on the new platform are co-authored with Claude.

On September 28 we moved the live product to the new platform overnight, together with its database. Fourteen agents checked production right after the switch. In the next 37 days we shipped 43 releases.
