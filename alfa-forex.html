The job

Alfa-Forex was Alfa-Bank’s trading platform for stocks, commodities and CFDs. I joined as lead designer with two tasks: bring the existing product up to date, and design a family of new retail platforms from scratch.

What I made

For Alfa-Forex I redesigned the dated screens on web and mobile and reworked the trading widgets and calculators on the old design system.

For the new platforms I built a component library with light and dark themes and designed onboarding, referrals and analytics dashboards. When outside help fell through, I made the 3D myself in Cinema 4D and Redshift.

What came of it

The second release added social features and a link to Alfa-Invest. Other Alfa products reused the components. Every platform passed the Central Bank’s compliance review.
